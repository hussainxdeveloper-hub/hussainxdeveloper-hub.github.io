Built different

Everything you need.
Nothing you don't.

Most people use three or four separate apps for notes, passwords, private photos, and task management. MonoNote replaces all of them — one app, one subscription, everything private.

📝
Rich Notes Editor
Write your thoughts, ideas, and plans without fear. Bold, italic, underline, code blocks, quotes — everything a real editor needs. Lock individual notes with a PIN, export as PDF, pin your most important ones to the top, and set reminders so nothing gets forgotten. Your notes are yours — encrypted, private, and never shared.
✅
To-Do Lists
Create lists, add items, set reminders on individual tasks, and track your progress with a live completion bar. Pin any list to the top. Everything stays local and private.
🔐
Password Vault
A secure vault that unlocks with your PIN, master password, or biometrics. Your passwords are stored only on your device, encrypted with AES-256, and never sent anywhere without your permission.
🖼️
Safe Box
Import photos and videos into a completely hidden private folder inside MonoNote. The moment you import them, they vanish from your gallery. No file manager or gallery app can see them. Only you can access them inside MonoNote with your vault unlocked.
☁️
Cloud Sync
Keep your notes perfectly in sync across all your devices in real time. Powered by end-to-end encryption — we never see your data in transit or at rest. Enable or disable it anytime from Settings.
🔔
Reminders That Work
Set reminders on any note, any to-do list, or any individual item inside a list. All notifications are 100% local — nothing is sent to any server. They even reschedule automatically after a device restart.
🎨
Beautiful Themes
Six hand-crafted themes, each with a full dark and light mode. The Pitch Black theme is AMOLED optimized for true blacks that save battery. Switch themes anytime from Settings — your notes app should feel as good as it works.
⚡
Backup & Restore
Back up everything — notes, to-do lists, passwords, and Safe Box media — with a single tap to a private Google Drive folder only MonoNote can access. Lost your phone? Switched devices? Restore everything exactly as it was, in one tap. Auto Backup handles it automatically for Premium users.

Built for privacy

Your data stays
yours. Always.

MonoNote was built from the ground up with encryption at every layer. Your passwords never leave your device unencrypted. Your Safe Box is invisible to every other app. Even we — the people who built MonoNote — cannot see what is inside your vault.

  • 🛡️
    AES-256-CBC encryption
    All vault data encrypted with randomly generated IVs per call.
  • 🔑
    End-to-end encrypted sync
    Passwords are encrypted on-device before leaving. Supabase never holds plaintext.
  • 👁️
    Biometric app lock
    Lock the entire app or just the vault with fingerprint or face ID (Premium).
  • 🗂️
    Row-Level Security (RLS)
    Supabase database rules enforce that users can only access their own data.
  • 📁
    Private media storage
    .nomedia file hides Safe Box from every gallery and file manager on your phone.
